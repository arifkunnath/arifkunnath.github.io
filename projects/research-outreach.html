Research Outreach Announcements

A from-scratch replacement for a third-party mailing tool. The research outreach team imports organisations and contacts, builds recipient lists, writes rich HTML announcements and sends them through university email, with full delivery logs.

Overview

What it does

  • Organisations with contacts, not a flat list, imported from CSV with provisional records for missing parents.
  • Static and filter-based lists; filters are evaluated at send time.
  • Safe sending: dedupe by normalised email, exclude unsubscribes and invalid addresses, then snapshot the audience.
  • Background batches with controlled concurrency, retries for transient failures and live progress counters.
  • Mail merge, test sends to yourself, a simple unsubscribe link and an audit log.
