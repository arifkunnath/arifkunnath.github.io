Certificate & Survey Portal

A self-service platform that lets university event managers publish a feedback form, design a certificate, and email every participant a personalised PDF the moment they submit. It replaces a set of hard-coded SharePoint 2019 web parts with one configurable system.

Overview

What it does

  • No code per event. A super admin and event managers configure everything from the app: settings, form, certificate, email and access.
  • Drag-and-drop form builder with short text, choices, rating grids and sections, plus a live phone/desktop preview.
  • Certificate designer on an A4 canvas: upload a background, place text boxes, insert placeholders like {Full Name} and {Issue Date}, and mix right-to-left Arabic with English.
  • Delivery you can trust. Each response produces a numbered PDF and an email; failures are counted and can be retried in one click.
  • Dashboard with charts per question, a date filter in local time, and CSV / Excel export.
  • Clone an event to reuse a previous form and certificate for the next workshop.
