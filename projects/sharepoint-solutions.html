Overview

What it does

  • Request to Recruit: a hiring-request workflow that passes through department heads, deans and directors, budget, vice chancellors and HR, with views and reports for each role.
  • Community Database: contact registration, search, history, export, and dashboards with charts by emirate, education and entity.
  • Upgraded across SharePoint versions and kept running alongside newer .NET and Angular systems.
  • Experience here shaped the newer portals: one rules table for workflows, and configuration instead of code.
