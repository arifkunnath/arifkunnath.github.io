UoS Mobile App

A single Flutter app for iOS and Android that adapts to who is signed in: public visitors see admissions, programmes and the academic calendar; students see their courses, grades, finance and digital ID; staff see their own tools.

Overview

What it does

  • Microsoft sign-in with role detection, so the dashboard and menu change per persona.
  • Student information: profile, courses and schedule, attendance, grades, dorm, e-services and finance.
  • Digital ISIC card flow: profile form, photo upload and crop, card display with offline cache, and discounts.
  • Push notifications with topics per audience, a notification history, and a small web admin to send them.
  • Built for everyone: Arabic and English, theme and font-size settings, an offline banner, and version checks.
  • Feature-first architecture (application / data / domain / presentation) so each module can grow on its own.
