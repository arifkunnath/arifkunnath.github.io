Ilan & Halin Adventures

Two brothers explore the world together. The big brother runs, jumps and swims to collect animal and planet facts, which are read aloud. His little brother tags along as a companion and saves the day with his magic at the "Help!" spots. I built it so both my boys could play, and see themselves, in the same game.

Overview

What it does

  • Made for a young player: no enemies, no lives and no game over. Fall into a gap and both brothers pop back onto the last safe ledge.
  • An AI companion who follows, copies jumps a moment later, catches up if he gets stuck, and claps and cheers at the right moments.
  • Five worlds, five ways to move: run and jump in the jungle, swim on the reef, float in low gravity, bounce on desert dunes and ride a giraffe lift on the savanna.
  • Facts read aloud with the browser's speech, collected into an Explorer Journal; a gold star on the world map for each finished world.
  • Every image and sound is original: cartoon characters, animals and backgrounds are drawn as SVG in code, and the music and sound effects are synthesised.
  • Kid-proofed by tests: unit tests check every fact orb and ledge is reachable, and browser tests play through each world automatically before every deploy.
